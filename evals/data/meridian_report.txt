About the company
Meridian Payments Ltd was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Pune, India. The company provides digital payment infrastructure to online and offline merchants, consumer wallets, cross-border remittances and embedded lending partners. As of 31 March 2025 Meridian employed 1,240 people across four offices. Anika Rao has served as Chief Executive Officer since 2019, and Vikram Sethi is the Chief Financial Officer. This report covers the financial and operating performance of the group for the year.

=== PAGE 2 ===
Financial highlights
Revenue for FY2025 was INR 4,820 crore, an increase of 18% over FY2024 revenue of INR 4,085 crore. Net profit rose to INR 612 crore from INR 498 crore in the previous year. EBITDA margin improved to 21.4%, compared with 19.8% in FY2024. Operating cash flow was INR 745 crore. Management attributes the growth mainly to higher merchant volumes and the rollout of new cross-border corridors during the year. Cost discipline in cloud infrastructure and customer support also contributed to the margin improvement.

=== PAGE 3 ===
Revenue by business segment
Merchant acquiring was the largest segment with revenue of INR 2,410 crore, which is 50% of total revenue. The consumer wallet segment contributed INR 1,205 crore (25%). Cross-border payments contributed INR 723 crore (15%) and Lending-as-a-service contributed INR 482 crore (10%). Cross-border revenue grew 31% year on year, the fastest of any segment, while Lending-as-a-service grew 12%. The wallet segment grew 9% as customer acquisition costs were kept flat.

Operating metrics
During FY2025 the platform processed 9.6 billion transactions with a total payment value of INR 14.2 lakh crore. Platform uptime was 99.97% and the average API response latency was 142 ms. The fraud loss rate was 0.011% of payment value, the lowest in the company's history. Peak throughput reached 18,500 transactions per second on 12 October 2024 during the festive shopping season, and the platform handled the spike without degradation.

Risk, security and compliance
Meridian received its payment aggregator authorisation from the Reserve Bank of India in August 2023. The company achieved PCI DSS v4.0 certification in January 2025 and renewed its ISO 27001 certificate in 2024. Production workloads run active-active from two data centres, located in Mumbai and Hyderabad. The company recorded 3 major (P1) incidents in FY2025, compared with 7 in FY2024. Each incident was followed by a published root-cause review.

Outlook and capital allocation
For FY2026 management guides to revenue growth of 20% to 23%. The company plans to hire 300 engineers and to launch UPI Credit in the second quarter of FY2026. Expansion into the UAE and Singapore is planned for the second half of the year. Planned capital expenditure is INR 260 crore, mostly for data centre capacity and security tooling. The board has proposed a dividend of INR 4.50 per share for FY2025.

Governance and people
The board has nine directors, of whom five are independent. The audit committee is chaired by Meera Iyer. The statutory auditor is Dalal & Pawar LLP. In FY2025, 38% of electricity used across offices and data centres came from renewable sources. Employee attrition fell to 14.2% from 17.5% in FY2024, and women make up 34% of the workforce.
